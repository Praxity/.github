# Praxity

The free toolchain for learning designers and their AI agents.

For learning designers who care about quality, accessibility and control.

## Tools

- [Studio](https://praxity.io/en/studio/). A Mac app for crafting self-paced courses, with SCORM and HTML export. [Releases](https://github.com/Praxity/desktop/releases)
- [Check](https://praxity.io/en/check/). Finds accessibility barriers in exported courses, interactive sites and PDFs. [Source](https://github.com/Praxity/praxity-check)
- [Trace](https://praxity.io/en/trace/). Charts how a course is built, from constructive alignment to clarity. [Source](https://github.com/Praxity/praxity-trace)
- [Proof](https://praxity.io/en/proof/). Collects learner results in your own Cloudflare account. [Source](https://github.com/Praxity/praxity-proof)
- Import. Moves Rise, Storyline, Captivate and other exports into Studio's format. In development.
- Onhand. Performance learning alongside your cursor. In development, see the [roadmap](https://praxity.io/en/roadmap/).

Studio courses use the open [.prax format](https://github.com/Praxity/prax-format), plain text you can read, edit and move.

## Workflows

- [Move a course out of Rise](https://praxity.io/en/workflows/migrate/). Import a Rise, Storyline or Captivate course into Studio, then check quality, find the fixes it needs and improve it.
- [Check every revision, with or without AI](https://praxity.io/en/workflows/iterate/). Trace and Check show where a draft is weak. You decide what changes.
- [Get a better first draft from your agent](https://praxity.io/en/workflows/agents/). Your agent checks its own work with Trace and Check before you see it.

Automated checks don't replace an expert review or lived experience.

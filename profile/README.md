# Praxity

Free tools for accessible eLearning.

Praxity helps you author interactive courses, check them for accessibility barriers before learners meet them, and measure results in infrastructure you control.

## Tools

- **[Studio](https://praxity.io/en/studio/)** — Author interactive, accessible courses on your Mac. Local-first, with SCORM and standalone HTML export. In development.
- **[Accessibility Audit](https://praxity.io/en/audit/)** — Check exported courses and interactive websites for accessibility barriers with a local command-line tool. [Source](https://github.com/Praxity/praxity-audit)
- **[Proof](https://praxity.io/en/proof/)** — Measure participation, completion, scores, and drop-off in an instance you deploy to your own Cloudflare account. [Source](https://github.com/Praxity/prax-proof)
- **Onhand** — Performance learning alongside your cursor. In development; [follow the roadmap](https://praxity.io/en/#roadmap).

Underneath the tools, the open [Prax format](https://github.com/Praxity/prax-format) keeps learning content portable.

## What we care about

- Accessibility tested in real use, with known limits stated plainly.
- Local-first and operator-controlled data instead of suite lock-in.
- Portable content and exports that remain useful without Praxity services.
- Clear project boundaries: every repository states its own licence.
